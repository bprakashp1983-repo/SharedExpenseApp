SharedExpense is a simple iOS app that helps groups of people — friends, family, or colleagues — track and split shared costs fairly.

You create a group (like "Weekend Trip" or "Apartment"), add the members, and log expenses as they happen. Each expense records who paid and how the cost is divided — either equally among everyone or with custom amounts per person. The app then calculates the net balance for each person and suggests the minimum number of payments needed to settle everything up, so nobody has to do the mental math.

Core features:

Create multiple groups with custom names and emoji icons
Add/remove members per group
Log expenses with title, amount, date, payer, and optional note
Split costs equally or enter custom amounts per person
Balances tab shows who owes money and who gets money back
Smart settlement suggestions — minimizes the number of transactions to clear all debts
All data saved locally on the device, no account or internet required
